- We're going to keep this file as a documentation of the thought/research
process we go through while designing our small transformer model. 

- After discussing the proposal further with Eoin, we decided that instead of changing several parts of the transformer at once,
 it would make more sense to focus on one specific part of the model and 
 study how different ways of building that part affect the prediction results.

- At the moment, we are leaning towards focusing on positional information. 
Since our model will be given the previous 10 matches for each team, the order 
of those matches could be important. For example, the most recent match may be more 
useful than a match from 10 games ago.

- One idea is to compare a transformer with no positional information against 
versions that do include positional information. 
We could then compare a few different approaches, such as fixed positional encoding, 
learned positional embeddings, or possibly a relative positional method.

- We are not deciding exactly which methods to use yet. 
The next step will be to research the different options properly and 
decide which ones make the most sense for a small transformer trained from scratch.
